Data Deletion - Final Capsule

This page explains how users of the Final Capsule app can delete selected profile information without deleting their entire account.

Important: Users do not need to delete their account to remove optional profile information. Profile information can be removed directly from the Final Capsule app.

How to Delete Profile Data

Users can remove their optional profile information directly from the Final Capsule app by following these steps:

  1. Open the Final Capsule app.
  2. Log in to your account.
  3. Open Profile.
  4. Remove the information you no longer want to store.
  5. Tap Save Profile.
After saving: The information removed from the profile will be updated in the account profile data.

Profile Data That Can Be Deleted

The following optional profile information can be removed without deleting the user's account:

  • Name
  • Mobile or phone number
  • Session information
  • Registration information
  • College or institution information

Data That Is Not Deleted by Removing Profile Information

Removing profile information does not delete the user's account, authentication information, or transaction and purchase records. These types of information are handled separately.

Purchase and Transaction Information

Purchase or transaction-related information may be retained when necessary for legitimate purposes such as maintaining transaction records, preventing fraud, resolving disputes, accounting, security, or complying with applicable legal requirements.

Retention: Information that is required to be retained will be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose and will be deleted or anonymized when it is no longer required, where applicable.

Account Deletion

If you want to permanently delete your account and associated account data instead of deleting only selected profile information, please use the Delete Account option in the Final Capsule app.

For detailed information about account deletion, please refer to the Final Capsule Account Deletion page.

Need Help?

If you are unable to remove your profile information or need assistance, please contact the Final Capsule support team through the support option provided in the app.
